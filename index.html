Questions

The fine print, without the fine print.

Is it really free?

Yes. We don't charge for design or building. If you want your own domain name (like yourbusiness.com), that's typically about $10 to $15 a year, paid directly to the provider. We'll help you set it up.

Who builds it?

A small team of trained Bridge students. Every site is reviewed by a student lead before it goes live.

How long does it take?

Usually three to six weeks from our first call to launch, depending on how many pages you need and how quickly we get your content.

What do I need to provide?

Your logo, a few photos, your business details (hours, address, services), and about an hour of your time across the project.

What happens after launch?

We walk you through making simple updates yourself, like changing hours or adding photos, and we're available for questions after launch.

Who is eligible?

Small businesses and nonprofits across Metro Atlanta. Not sure if you qualify? Apply anyway and we'll talk.

About

Students who got tired of waiting to learn the good stuff.

Our story

School taught us how to pass tests. The world kept asking whether we could build a website, edit a video, or explain an idea to a room full of people.

So we started teaching each other. Bridge Innovation began as a few students sharing what they'd taught themselves, and grew into free workshops, tutoring, seminars, and hackathons for middle and high school students.

Along the way we noticed something else: local businesses needed websites, and our students needed real projects. Now we build those sites for free, and everyone comes out ahead.
